PIH Guard

Catches dark marks early after laser, peel or RF microneedling.

1The clinic sets up your procedure. Skin type, device and settings, filled in at the visit.
2You take one flash photo each night. Lights off, about 40 seconds.
3If healing drifts, the app steps in. It tells you what to do, checks every day if it works, and lets the clinic know.

Concept for the Stanford AI Longevity Module 2 discussion. Readings are uncalibrated and the forecast is a teaching model. Not a medical device.

Step 2 of 2 · At home

Your nightly photo

Same light every night is what makes the numbers comparable.

1Turn off the lights.
2Put the gray color sticker next to the treated area.
3Flash on. Hold the phone about 30 cm away, same angle each night.
4Tap the treated spot, then normal skin next to it. That's it.

Photos stay on this phone. Only the numbers are saved.
